[h2] Questions
[span] What is included in an End of Tenancy Cleaning?
[p] Our end of tenancy clean covers every room top to bottom, kitchen appliances, oven, fridge, bathrooms, limescale removal, skirting boards, inside cupboards, carpets, and more. We follow a comprehensive checklist accepted by letting agents and landlords across Central Scotland.
[span] How long does a deep clean typically take?
[p] A standard deep clean for a 2-bedroom property usually takes 4–6 hours. Larger properties or those requiring extra attention may take longer. We always stay until every item on the checklist is completed.
[span] Are your cleaning teams fully insured?
[p] Yes, all our teams are fully insured and vetted. We carry comprehensive public liability insurance on every job, so you can have complete peace of mind when we're in your home.
[span] Do you offer last-minute or same-day bookings?
[p] Absolutely. We understand that sometimes you need a clean at short notice. We accept last-minute bookings subject to availability, contact us directly for urgent requests and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
[span] Do you supply all cleaning equipment and products?
[p] Yes, all equipment and professional-grade cleaning supplies are included in our price. You don't need to provide anything, our teams arrive fully equipped and ready to work.
